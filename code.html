AI Image Slideshow — How It Works

Your dream description is broken into 4–6 key scenes. Each scene is sent to an AI image generator which creates a still frame. These frames are assembled into an animated slideshow with dreamy crossfade transitions — giving you a cinematic "dream reel" in under 30 seconds.

Cost: ~$0.02–0.08 per dream. Free tier available.

AI Video Generation — How It Works

Your dream is processed into a cinematic prompt and sent to an AI video model. A 5–15 second video clip is generated with real motion, atmosphere, and lighting that matches your dream's mood. Results can be stunning — and surreal in exactly the right way.

Cost: ~$0.25–1.50 per dream. Credits required.
